# How to Use Fire Claim Examiner

## What This App Does

You give it a claim. It reads the claim against the fire policy and tells you three things: whether the policy pays, which clause decides it, and how much to pay.

It shows its working. Every amount is broken down item by item, and every decision points to the exact words in the policy it came from.

It does not decide the claim. You do. It gives you the grounds.

## How to Start

1. Double-click `open-app.bat`. The app starts and opens in your browser.
2. Wait for the green "ready" light at the top. The first start takes about half a minute while the policy loads.
3. The Claim Desk screen opens by itself.

## How to Use It

**Step 1. Pick a claim.**
Six claims are already loaded. Click one. You can also paste a claim record into the box on the right if you want to test your own.

**Step 2. Check the endorsement list.**
The app shows the endorsements it thinks the customer holds. This comes from the claim record, not from the policy. Check it against your customer system and correct it if it is wrong. This is the one thing the app cannot look up for you.

**Step 3. Read the verdict.**
You will get one of three answers:

- **Covered.** The policy pays. A breakdown shows how much.
- **Not covered.** The policy does not pay. The clause that stops it is quoted.
- **Escalate.** The wording can be read more than one way. The app tells you the exact question a senior examiner needs to answer.

**Step 4. Read the breakdown.**
For a covered claim you get a table. One row per item claimed. Each row shows the clause that lets it in or keeps it out, any deduction applied, and the running total. The bottom line is the recommended amount.

**Step 5. Check the working if you want to.**
Click "Under the hood" on any step. You will see which parts of the policy the app read, which one it decided was in charge, and why it beat the others. Nothing is hidden.

**Step 6. Copy it onto the file.**
Use the copy button. It copies the reasoning and the breakdown as plain text.

## Main Features

- **Reads the dates first.** Before anything else, it checks the loss happened inside the policy period. A claim outside the period stops there.
- **Knows which clause wins.** When two clauses disagree, endorsements override the base policy. The app says which one governs and which one it displaced.
- **Checks the conditions, not just the peril.** Holding the right endorsement is not enough. The app tests what actually happened against that endorsement's own conditions.
- **Does the arithmetic properly.** Excesses, exclusions and underinsurance are worked out in a fixed order by the calculator, not guessed. The same claim always gives the same number.
- **Refuses when it should.** Ask it about a premium, a policy number or whether a claim was paid, and it will tell you that lives in the records system.
- **Scores itself.** The Evaluation screen runs all six claims against a set of known answers and shows where it was right and where it was wrong.

## Tips

- If the answer looks wrong, check the endorsement list first. Nearly every wrong answer traces back to a wrong endorsement going in.
- "Escalate" is a real answer, not a failure. It means the wording is genuinely unclear, and it tells you what to ask.
- The app only knows this one policy document. It does not know your company's practice, past claims, or what was agreed with the customer.
- The Evaluation screen is the honest one. Look at it before you trust the app on a live file.

## Saving Your Work

Nothing is saved. Every claim you run is worked out fresh and disappears when you close the browser. No account, no login, no customer data stored anywhere.

Use the copy button to keep anything you need.
