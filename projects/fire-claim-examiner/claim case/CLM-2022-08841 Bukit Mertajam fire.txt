CIRCUMSTANCE OF LOSS
=====================================================================
The claimant states that a fire broke out in the rear storeroom on the
ground floor in the early hours of 28 July 2022. The premises were closed
for business at the time. A neighbour occupying the adjoining lot noticed
smoke and alerted the Fire and Rescue Department, who attended at
approximately 0240 hours and extinguished the fire by 0405 hours.

The claimant states the fire consumed shelving and stock in the rear
storeroom, and that heat and smoke spread up the internal staircase to
the first floor storage area. The rear section of the clay tile roof
collapsed during firefighting operations. The front retail area sustained
smoke staining throughout.

The claimant states the likely origin was an electrical fault at a
distribution board in the storeroom. A Fire and Rescue Department report
dated 3 August 2022 records the cause as undetermined but notes burn
patterns consistent with an origin at or near the distribution board.

The claimant advises that his father, the named Insured, retired from the
business in March 2022 and that the claimant has since operated the shop
in his own name. The claimant states he continued paying the annual
premium by standing instruction and believed the policy was current. A
renewal notice for the period following 31 January 2021 was issued by the
Company to the Insured's registered address; no renewal premium receipt
signed by an official or appointed agent of the Company has been produced
by the claimant to date.

Business registration records produced by the claimant show the sole
proprietorship was transferred from Tan Cheng Hock to Tan Wei Ming with
effect from 1 March 2022. No endorsement recording a change of insured
interest appears on the policy.
