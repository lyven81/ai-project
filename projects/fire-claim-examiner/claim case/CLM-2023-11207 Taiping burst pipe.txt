CIRCUMSTANCE OF LOSS
=====================================================================
The Insured states that on the morning of 2 December 2023 she attended the
premises and found standing water across the ground floor retail area to a
depth of approximately 40mm, with water still running down the rear wall
and dripping from the ground floor ceiling.

A plumber engaged by the Insured on the same day traced the source to a
concealed 15mm copper supply pipe serving the first floor washroom. The
pipe had failed at a soldered joint within the wall cavity. The plumber's
invoice records that the joint and adjacent pipework were heavily
corroded, and that a section of pipe was replaced.

The Insured states the water ran unnoticed overnight and possibly for
longer, as the ground floor was not in daily use at the time. Water
damaged the ground floor suspended ceiling, wall plaster and skirting, and
soaked cartons of stationery and office files stored at floor level in the
rear of the ground floor area.

Tenancy records produced by the Insured show that the previous tenant of
the ground floor retail lot, Syarikat Perabot Kota, ended its tenancy and
handed back keys on 15 October 2023. The Insured states the lot was
advertised for letting from late October and that no new tenant had taken
occupation as at the date of loss. The Insured states she visited the
premises roughly once a week during this period to check on it and to
collect mail.

The Insured has also produced a plumbing inspection report dated
19 September 2023, prepared by Syarikat Paip Aman at her own request
following a complaint of low water pressure. That report notes visible
green corrosion staining on exposed pipework at the first floor washroom,
records that concealed sections were likely to be in similar condition,
and recommends replacement of the washroom supply pipework "as soon as
practicable". The Insured states she obtained a quotation for the work in
October 2023 but deferred it pending a new tenancy, and that the work had
not been carried out as at the date of loss.

The first floor washroom itself sustained no damage other than to the
replaced pipework. There is no automatic sprinkler installation at the
premises.
