CIRCUMSTANCE OF LOSS
=====================================================================
The Insured states that she closed the shop at approximately 2200 hours on
14 July 2021 and retired to the first floor residence. On descending at
approximately 0630 hours on 15 July 2021 she found the rear service door
open, the folding steel grille forced at the lower track, and the shop in
disarray.

The Insured states that cigarettes, canned goods, cooking oil and infant
formula were taken from the shelves and from the rear store, and that the
cash drawer under the front counter had been prised open and emptied. She
states the drawer held approximately RM 9,500 in notes and coin, being
takings from 12 to 14 July 2021 which she had not yet banked in.

A police report was lodged at Balai Polis Patani Road on 15 July 2021 at
0912 hours, report number GTN/007744/21. The report records forced entry
and theft of goods and cash, and remains under investigation.

The Insured states there was no fire, no explosion and no smoke at the
premises at any time before, during or after the incident, and that the
only damage to the building was to the rear service door, its frame and
the folding steel grille.

The Insured states she did not contact the Company immediately because
she believed the matter was one for the police, and only attended the
Company's branch office on 2 August 2021 after her son suggested that the
shop policy might respond.

The Insured has produced a policy schedule showing the period 30 July 2021
to 29 July 2022. She states that the shop has been continuously insured
with the Company for several years under successive annual policies and
that she does not retain copies of the earlier schedules. No schedule for
any period preceding 30 July 2021 has been produced, and no such policy
appears on this file.

The premises were occupied throughout by the Insured and her family. There
has been no change of trade or occupation and no transfer of interest in
the property.
