CIRCUMSTANCE OF LOSS
=====================================================================
The claimant states that a severe thunderstorm passed over Taman Cempaka
on the late afternoon of 30 June 2022, accompanied by sustained strong
winds. A Malaysian Meteorological Department record for the Ipoh station
on that date, produced by the claimant, notes a thunderstorm with maximum
gusts of 68 km/h between 1720 and 1815 hours.

The claimant states that the wind lifted and displaced a section of clay
roof tiles along the rear slope of the main roof, and that two timber
battens were broken. A section of the rear gable wall coping was also
dislodged. Photographs taken on the evening of 30 June show tiles absent
from the rear slope and daylight visible into the roof space.

The claimant states that rain then entered the roof space through the
opening created by the displaced tiles and came through the ceiling of
the rear bedroom and the adjoining passage, bringing down two ceiling
boards and soaking the bedroom floor, a wardrobe, a mattress and bedding.
Water staining is visible on the passage ceiling in the photographs.

The claimant further states that during the same storm a branch from a
mature rain tree standing on the neighbouring vacant lot broke off and
fell across the car porch, cracking three translucent roof sheets and
denting the porch gutter. The branch was removed by the local council on
2 July 2022.

The claimant also claims for a metal signboard bearing the family name
mounted on the front boundary wall, and a retractable fabric awning over
the front windows, both of which were torn from their fixings by the wind
and are beyond repair.

The dwelling was occupied throughout. The Insured's spouse was at home at
the time of the storm. There has been no change in the occupation or use
of the building, and no transfer of interest in the property. The Insured
remains the registered owner.

Emergency tarpaulin covering was placed over the rear roof slope on
1 July 2022 to prevent further water ingress.
