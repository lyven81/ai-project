CIRCUMSTANCE OF LOSS
=====================================================================
The claimant states that continuous heavy rain fell across the Kampar
district from the afternoon of 19 December 2023 through the night. Sungai
Kampar overtopped its banks in the early hours of 20 December 2023 and
water spread through the town centre.

The claimant states water entered the ground floor of the premises from
the rear lane at approximately 0300 hours on 20 December 2023 and rose to
a depth of approximately 1.2 metres, remaining at that level until the
afternoon of 21 December before receding. The claimant and her two
children moved to the first floor and were not injured.

A Department of Irrigation and Drainage flood bulletin for the Kampar
district dated 20 December 2023, produced by the claimant, records the
Sungai Kampar water level as having exceeded the danger threshold between
0140 and 1600 hours on that date.

The claimant states the water carried silt and debris through the shop,
destroying stock stored on lower shelving and at floor level, soaking wall
plaster and cabinetry to a height of approximately 1.3 metres, and ruining
a chest freezer, a display chiller and the shop's point of sale terminal.
Personal effects stored in a ground floor rear room, including clothing
and household linen, were also destroyed.

The claimant further states that as the water receded, a section of the
rear boundary wall approximately four metres in length leaned and partly
collapsed, and that the paved rear driveway settled unevenly. She states a
contractor attributed this to the ground beneath having been saturated and
washed out. The main building structure was not affected by this
settlement and shows no cracking.

The claimant also claims for cash of RM 6,800 kept in a tin in a drawer
beneath the shop counter, being takings from 16 to 19 December 2023 which
had not been banked in before the flood.

The claimant states that she did not contact the Company earlier because
the town was without electricity and mobile coverage for several days, the
family was occupied with cleaning and with school arrangements for the
children, and the Insured was in Singapore until the new year. She states
she first attended the Company's branch office on 20 January 2024. No
request for an extension of time to submit the claim was made to the
Company before that date, and no written extension appears on this file.

The premises were occupied throughout by the claimant and her children.
There has been no change of trade or occupation. The Insured remains the
registered proprietor of the premises.
