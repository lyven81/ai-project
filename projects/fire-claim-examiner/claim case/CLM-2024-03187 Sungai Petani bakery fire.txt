CIRCUMSTANCE OF LOSS
=====================================================================
The claimant states that the bakery closed at approximately 2200 hours on
13 September 2024 after the evening production run, and that all staff had
left the premises by 2230 hours. Nobody was on the premises overnight; the
first floor is used for storage and office work only and nobody resides
there.

At approximately 0140 hours on 14 September 2024 a passer-by reported
smoke issuing from the shopfront. The Fire and Rescue Department attended
at 0158 hours and declared the fire extinguished at 0312 hours.

The claimant states the seat of the fire was at the spiral dough mixer
standing against the rear wall of the production area. A Fire and Rescue
Department report dated 19 September 2024 records the probable cause as an
electrical fault, and notes severe localised burning at the mixer with
fire spread outward from that position.

An independent electrical report dated 23 September 2024, commissioned by
the claimant from a registered competent person, records that the mixer's
motor windings show evidence of short circuiting and overheating
consistent with an insulation failure within the motor, and that the
motor's own damage is attributable to that electrical fault rather than to
external burning. The same report records that the premises wiring,
distribution board and other machines showed no pre-existing defect, and
that their damage is consistent with exposure to the fire.

The claimant states the fire spread from the mixer to adjacent timber
shelving and packaging materials, then across the production area,
damaging a deck oven, a dough proofer and two stainless steel worktables,
and burning through part of the ground floor ceiling. Smoke and soot
affected the whole ground floor and the lower part of the internal
staircase. The first floor storage area sustained smoke staining only.

Stock held in the production area and the ground floor store, comprising
flour, sugar, butter, fillings and printed packaging, was destroyed by
fire, heat or contamination and was disposed of under the supervision of
the local authority health officer on 16 September 2024.

The claimant states there has been no change in the trade carried on at
the premises, no change of occupation, and no transfer of interest in the
property or the business. The bakery has operated continuously at this
address since 2016.

A valuation report dated 24 September 2024, prepared by a registered
valuer instructed by the claimant, assesses values at the premises
immediately before the loss as follows: building reinstatement value
RM 275,000.00; contents, plant and machinery reinstatement value
RM 200,000.00; stock in trade at cost RM 52,000.00.
