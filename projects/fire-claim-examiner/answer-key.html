Claim 1: CLM-2022-08841, Bukit Mertajam fire

Decline

Policy FP/PG/2021/004512 . Period 1 Jan 2021 to 31 Jan 2021 . Loss 28 Jul 2022 . Claimed RM 295,700

No cover. The loss occurred outside the period of insurance.

Primary ground

The policy period expired on 31 January 2021. The loss occurred on 28 July 2022, roughly eighteen months after expiry. No renewal schedule or endorsement extending the period appears on the file, and no premium receipt signed by an official or duly appointed agent of the Company has been produced.

Condition 2: "No payment in respect of any premium shall be deemed to be payment to the Company unless a printed form of receipt for the same signed by an Official or duly appointed Agent of the Company shall have been given to the Insured."

A bank standing instruction is not proof of renewal under Condition 2. The claimant's belief that cover was current does not create cover.

Secondary ground, to be stated even though the primary ground disposes of the claim

The business was transferred from the named Insured to the claimant with effect from 1 March 2022, with no endorsement recording the change.

Condition 9(d): the insurance ceases to attach "if the interest in the property insured pass from the Insured otherwise than by will or operation of law" unless the sanction of the Company is obtained by endorsement before the loss.
Trap the assistant must avoid

The peril is fire, which is the core insured peril, and the fire itself is well evidenced. An assistant that reasons from peril to cover will approve this claim. The period check must run before the peril check.

Claim 2: CLM-2023-11207, Taiping burst pipe

Decline

Policy FP/PK/2023/001938 . Period 1 Jan 2023 to 31 Dec 2023 . Loss 2 Dec 2023 . Claimed RM 30,900

In period, correct endorsement held, but two independent conditions of that endorsement are not met.

Endorsement correctly identified

FP507B applies, not FP507A: the building is two storeys with no mezzanine, and FP507A is limited to buildings exceeding five storeys including mezzanine. FP504 does not reach this loss, because its definition requires water "originating from outside the building insured"; this was a concealed supply pipe within the wall cavity.

Ground one, premises untenanted
FP507B excludes "(a) loss or damage caused whilst the premises are untenanted."

The ground floor retail lot was handed back on 15 October 2023 and remained unlet on 2 December 2023, a period of 48 days. The damage claimed is to the ground floor. Weekly visits by the Insured to check the property do not constitute tenancy.

Condition 9(b) is a parallel ground: cover ceases where the building "becomes unoccupied and so remains for a period of more than thirty (30) days" without the Company's sanction endorsed on the policy.

Ground two, known defect not remedied
FP507 Special Condition 3: the Insured "shall use all reasonable diligence and care to keep the premises in a proper state of repair and if any defect therein be discovered shall cause such defect to be made good as soon as possible... the Company shall not be liable for any loss or damage caused by a defect which the Insured has failed to remedy after having received notice of such defect either from the Company or any person or public body."

The inspection report of 19 September 2023 recorded corrosion on the washroom pipework and recommended replacement as soon as practicable. A quotation was obtained in October 2023 and the work was deferred. The failure occurred at a corroded joint on that same pipework.

Point of genuine difficulty, which a strong answer should acknowledge

Special Condition 3 speaks of notice "from the Company or any person or public body". The report was commissioned by the Insured from her own contractor. Whether a privately engaged plumber is "any person" within the clause is arguable. The determination does not rest on it: ground one stands independently. A good answer flags this rather than asserting the point.

Had the claim been payable
FP507B excess: the first RM 1,000.00 of each and every loss at each separate premises. Reduction to 1% (minimum RM 100) applies only where the sum insured is under RM 50,000. Total sums insured here are RM 383,000, so the flat RM 1,000 excess would apply.

Claim 3: CLM-2022-06033, Ipoh windstorm

Approve, with two items excluded and part routed to a second endorsement

Policy FP/PK/2022/007745 . Period 2 Jun 2022 to 1 Jun 2023 . Loss 30 Jun 2022 . Claimed RM 55,850

Payable. This is the one approved claim of the five.

Why cover responds

Condition 6(b) excludes atmospheric disturbance from the base policy, and FP503 overrides it. FP503 is held.

FP503 Special Condition 1: the Company is not liable for damage by water or rain "unless the building insured... shall first sustain actual damage to the roof or walls of same by the direct force of Hurricane, Cyclone, Typhoon and Windstorm and shall then be liable only for such damage to the interior... as may be caused by water or rain entering the building through openings in the roof or walls made by the direct force of the said perils."

The sequence is satisfied on the facts: wind displaced roof tiles and broke battens, dislodging the gable coping, and rain then entered through those openings. Timestamped photographs and the meteorological record for the Ipoh station support the sequence. Condition 12 is satisfied: notice given on 1 July, written claim delivered 8 July, within fifteen days.

The claimant is not the named Insured, which is not fatal here. Written authorisation is on file and the Insured remains registered proprietor, so no transfer of interest engages Condition 9(d).

Items excluded
FP503 Special Condition 4(a): unless specifically and separately insured, the endorsement does not cover "Metal smoke stacks, awnings, blinds, signs or other outdoor fixtures or fittings of any description."

Item 6, the metal signboard on the boundary wall (RM 1,850), and item 7, the retractable fabric awning (RM 3,600), are outdoor fixtures. The Schedule carries no separate item for them. Both fall out.

Item routed to a different endorsement

Item 5, the car porch roof sheets and gutter (RM 4,300), was caused by a falling branch, not by direct wind force on the building. It is payable under FP513, which is held, and which carries its own excess of the first RM 250 of each and every claim. It should not be paid under FP503.

Indicative computation
FP503 excess: lesser of (a) 1% of total sums insured, or (b) RM 200. Total sums insured = RM 380,000 + RM 95,000 = RM 475,000 1% of RM 475,000 = RM 4,750 Lesser of the two = RM 200 Storm portion (items 1, 2, 3, 4, 8) RM 46,100 less FP503 excess RM 200 ----------- RM 45,900 Falling branch portion (item 5, under FP513) RM 4,300 less FP513 excess RM 250 ----------- RM 4,050 Excluded (items 6 and 7, FP503 SC 4(a)) RM 5,450 INDICATIVE PAYABLE RM 49,950

Quantum remains subject to the adjuster's assessment of each item and to any condition of average. Item 8, emergency tarpaulin (RM 950), is mitigation expenditure and should be flagged for confirmation rather than assumed; the policy provides expressly for fire fighting costs at Condition 13, not for storm mitigation. Reinstatement is on FC816 terms.

Claim 4: CLM-2021-05512, George Town burglary

Decline

Policy FP/PG/2021/006180 . Period 30 Jul 2021 to 29 Jul 2022 . Loss 15 Jul 2021 . Claimed RM 41,150

Three independent grounds, any one of which disposes of the claim.

Ground one, loss before inception

The policy incepted on 30 July 2021. The loss occurred on 15 July 2021, fifteen days earlier. No policy covering that date appears on the file and none has been produced. Continuous insurance in previous years is asserted but not evidenced, and would in any event require the earlier schedule.

Ground two, peril not insured

This is a fire policy. Burglary is not among the insured perils, and no endorsement extending cover to theft or burglary appears on the Schedule. FP512A and FP512B (Riot, Strike and Malicious Damage) are not held, and would not respond to burglary in any event.

Condition 5(1)(a): the insurance does not cover "Loss by theft during or after the occurrence of a fire."

Condition 5(1)(a) is frequently misread as the operative clause here. It is not. It addresses theft occurring in connection with a fire, and it is an exclusion carved out of fire cover. On these facts there was no fire at all, so theft is simply outside the scope of the insuring clause. A strong answer makes that distinction rather than citing 5(1)(a) as the reason for declinature.

Ground three, cash not insured
Condition 8(e): unless otherwise expressly stated, the insurance does not cover "Securities, obligations, or documents of any kind, stamps, coins or paper money, cheques, books of account or other business books, or computer systems records."

The RM 9,500 of notes and coin is excluded regardless of peril, as no money item appears on the Schedule.

Also worth noting

First notification was on 2 August 2021, eighteen days after the loss, with the written claim on 6 August. That breaches Condition 12 independently. It is a fourth ground and should be recorded, though the claim fails well before it.

Claim 5: CLM-2023-12440, Kampar flood

Decline

Policy FP/PK/2023/009021 . Period 1 Dec 2023 to 30 Nov 2024 . Loss 20 Dec 2023 . Claimed RM 110,750

The peril is squarely covered. The claim fails on a procedural condition precedent.

The peril would have responded

Condition 6 does not exclude flood. FP504 is held and the facts fall within its definition: overflow of a natural watercourse, water originating from outside the building. Sungai Kampar overtopped its banks and water entered from the rear lane. This is the clearest coverage position of the five claims, which is what makes it a test.

Why it nonetheless fails
Condition 12: on the happening of any loss the Insured "shall forthwith give notice thereof to the Company and shall within 15 days after the loss or damage, or such further time as the Company may in writing allow in that behalf, deliver to the Company" a written claim with particulars. It closes: "No claim under this Policy shall be payable unless the terms of this Condition have been complied with."

The loss occurred on 20 December 2023. First notification and the written claim were both on 20 January 2024, thirty-one days later. No request for an extension was made before that date and no written extension appears on the file. Condition 12 is a condition precedent to payment, and it is breached.

The claimant's explanation (loss of power and mobile coverage, the Insured abroad, family disruption) is relevant to whether the Company exercises its discretion to allow further time in writing, which it may still do. It does not cure the breach on its own. The correct output is a declinature on Condition 12 with the discretion flagged for a senior referral, not a silent refusal.

Items that would have failed even on a timely claim
  • Rear boundary wall and paved driveway (items 6 and 7, RM 12,900). FP504 Special Condition 1(c) excludes loss caused by subsidence or landslip. FP510 is held but excludes "swimming pools, terraces, patios, drives, footpaths, walls, gates or fences unless the building, its outbuildings or garages are damaged by the same cause and at the same time." The building was not damaged by the settlement. FP510 also carries an excess of 5% of the total sum insured or RM 25,000, whichever is lower, which exceeds the amount claimed for these items.
  • Cash of RM 6,800 (item 8). Excluded by Condition 8(e), as no money item appears on the Schedule.
Had the claim been timely
FP504 excess: lesser of (a) 1% of total sums insured, or (b) the first RM 2,500.00 of each and every loss. Total sums insured = RM 300,000 + RM 85,000 + RM 40,000 = RM 425,000 1% of RM 425,000 = RM 4,250 Lesser of the two = RM 2,500

Claim 6: CLM-2024-03187, Sungai Petani bakery fire

Approve, with the originating machine excluded and average applied to one item

Policy FP/KD/2024/002604 . Period 1 Mar 2024 to 28 Feb 2025 . Loss 14 Sep 2024 . Claimed RM 168,200

Payable RM 130,700. This is the second approved claim of the six.

Why cover responds

Fire is the core insured peril under the base policy. No endorsement is needed to bring the loss within cover, and none of the Condition 6 exclusions is engaged: this was an accidental electrical fire, not an atmospheric, seismic, war, riot or terrorism event. Condition 8(i) is not in point, as no bush or lalang burning is involved.

Condition 12 is satisfied: notice given 15 September, written claim delivered 25 September, eleven days after the loss. Condition 9 is not engaged: the trade is unchanged, the building was not unoccupied for more than thirty days (closing overnight is not unoccupancy), nothing was removed to another location, and the Insured remains registered proprietor, so the daughter lodging under written authorisation does not engage Condition 9(d).

Item excluded: the machine where the fault originated
FP508A.01: the Company is "free from liability for loss of or damage to, any electrical machine, apparatus, or any portion of the electrical installation arising from or occasioned by over-running, excessive pressure, short-circuiting, self heating, arcing or leakage of electricity from whatever cause (lightning included) arising. Provided that this exemption shall only apply to the particular electrical machine, apparatus, or portion of the electrical installation so affected, and not to other machines, apparatus or electrical installation destroyed or damaged by fire set up by such particular machine."

The independent electrical report of 23 September 2024 attributes the mixer's motor damage to insulation failure and short circuiting within the motor itself. Item 2 of the claim, the spiral dough mixer at RM 18,500, is therefore excluded.

The proviso is equally important and cuts the other way: the deck oven, proofer, worktables, shelving and packaging fittings were damaged by the fire the mixer started, and the same report records no pre-existing defect in them. They remain covered. An assistant that reads FP508A.01 as excluding all electrical loss will wrongly strip out the oven and proofer as well.

Condition 20, average, applies to one item only
Condition 20: "If the property hereby insured shall, at the breaking out of any fire, be collectively of greater value than the sum insured thereon, then the Insured shall be considered as being his own Insurer for the difference, and shall bear a rateable proportion of the loss accordingly. Every item, if more than one, of the Policy shall be separately subject to this Condition."

The closing sentence is the test. Average is applied item by item, never to the claim as a whole. Comparing the Schedule against the valuation report of 24 September 2024:

  • Item 1, Building. Sum insured RM 280,000 against reinstatement value RM 275,000. Adequately insured, no average.
  • Item 2, Contents, plant and machinery. Sum insured RM 150,000 against reinstatement value RM 200,000. Underinsured, average applies at 150,000 / 200,000 = 0.75.
  • Item 3, Stock in trade. Sum insured RM 60,000 against value RM 52,000. Adequately insured, no average.

Average is applied after the FP508A.01 exclusion, not before, since the excluded machine is not part of the loss the Company indemnifies.

Computation
ITEM 1 BUILDING (claim refs 1 and 6) Ceiling, wall finishes, rewiring, roof, repainting RM 46,000 Soot removal, cleaning and disposal RM 6,200 ----------- Sub-total RM 52,200 Average: SI 280,000 vs value 275,000, none n/a PAYABLE ITEM 1 RM 52,200 ITEM 2 CONTENTS, PLANT AND MACHINERY (refs 2, 3, 4) Spiral dough mixer RM 18,500 EXCLUDED, FP508A.01 Deck oven, proofer, worktables RM 62,000 Timber shelving, store fittings RM 14,000 ---------- Covered sub-total RM 76,000 Average: SI 150,000 / value 200,000 = 0.75 76,000 x 0.75 RM 57,000 PAYABLE ITEM 2 RM 57,000 ITEM 3 STOCK IN TRADE (claim ref 5) Flour, sugar, butter, fillings, packaging RM 21,500 Average: SI 60,000 vs value 52,000, none n/a PAYABLE ITEM 3 RM 21,500 Schedule excess for fire peril Nil =========== RECOMMENDED PAYABLE RM 130,700 Reconciliation of the RM 37,500 reduction: FP508A.01 exclusion, mixer RM 18,500 Condition 20 average on Item 2 (76,000 x 0.25) RM 19,000 ----------- RM 37,500

Reinstatement is on FC816 terms. Quantum of each item remains subject to the adjuster's assessment; the determination, the exclusion and the average factor are not.

Traps the assistant must avoid
  1. Applying average to the whole claim (168,200 or 149,700 as a single pool) instead of item by item. The one-item application is what Condition 20's closing sentence requires.
  2. Applying average to Items 1 and 3, which are adequately insured.
  3. Excluding the oven and proofer along with the mixer, against the express proviso in FP508A.01.
  4. Applying average before the exclusion, which would give RM 70,875 on Item 2 rather than RM 57,000.
  5. Applying an endorsement excess. This is a base-peril fire claim and the Schedule states no excess; the FP503, FP504, FP507 and FP513 excesses are specific to those endorsed perils.

What each case tests

  1. Temporal precedence. Claims 1 and 4 are void on the policy period, one after expiry and one before inception. Both describe vivid, well documented losses. The period check must run before any coverage reasoning.
  2. Endorsement held is not endorsement satisfied. Claim 2 holds exactly the right endorsement and still fails, on the endorsement's own exclusion and special condition. This is the case that separates clause retrieval from clause application.
  3. Authority and routing within one claim. Claim 3 requires three different treatments across eight items: FP503 for the storm damage, FP513 for the falling branch, and exclusion for the outdoor fixtures, each with its own excess.
  4. Covered peril, failed condition. Claim 5 is the inverse of claim 2. The peril could not be more clearly covered, and the claim still fails on Condition 12. An assistant that reasons only about perils will approve it.
  5. Correct clause for the right reason. Claim 4 invites a wrong citation, Condition 5(1)(a), when the real answer is that the peril is outside the insuring clause entirely. Scoring should mark citation accuracy separately from the determination.
  6. Honest uncertainty. Claim 2's Special Condition 3 turns on whether a privately engaged plumber is "any person" giving notice. The expected behaviour is to flag the ambiguity and rest the decision on the independent ground, not to assert a confident reading.
  7. Money and outdoor fixtures. Condition 8(e) and the outdoor fixtures exclusions recur across claims 3, 4 and 5, so an assistant cannot pass by learning one case.
  8. Two different kinds of arithmetic. Claim 3 is a flat-excess calculation with two different excesses running in parallel (RM 200 under FP503, RM 250 under FP513). Claim 6 is a proportional calculation under Condition 20, applied to one Schedule item and not the other two, after an exclusion rather than before it. An assistant that hard-codes one excess per claim fails both.
  9. A partial exclusion that also grants cover. FP508A.01 in claim 6 excludes the originating machine and, in the same sentence, expressly preserves cover for everything the resulting fire damaged. Reading only the first half of the clause strips out the oven and proofer and understates the payable by RM 57,000.
  10. Base peril without an endorsement. Claim 6 needs no endorsement to attach. Four of the six turn on endorsements, so an assistant may learn to look for one and then wrongly decline a claim that the base insuring clause already covers.
