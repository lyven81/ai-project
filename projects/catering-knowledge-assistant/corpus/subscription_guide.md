# 好吃厨房 Tasty Kitchen, Subscription Guide

We offer two plans only: the **daily plan** and the **monthly plan**. There is no weekly plan.

Both plans deliver the same food from the same weekly menu. The menu is fixed and the same for every customer on a given day. Customers do not choose their dish. The subscription is a convenience arrangement, not a menu choice: it saves you from ordering every day, and on the monthly plan it also saves you money on the RM18 meals.

## Daily plan

Order meal by meal, as often or as rarely as you like.

- Order any number of days: one day, three days, twelve days, or any pattern that suits you
- Choose lunch only, dinner only, or both
- Price: **RM15** or **RM18** per meal, as printed on the weekly menu
- The RM18 meals are usually the Wednesday and Thursday claypot or slow-braised dishes. The weekly menu prints the price of every meal
- Pay per use. Pay for the meals you order, when you order them
- No commitment, no minimum, no lock-in

## Monthly plan

A block of **20 weekday meals** for one service, starting on the date you choose.

- Price: **RM300** for 20 meals of one service, which works out to RM15 per meal
- Lunch only: RM300 per cycle
- Dinner only: RM300 per cycle
- Both lunch and dinner: RM600 per cycle, which is 20 lunches plus 20 dinners
- Your cycle starts on your own start date. It does not have to be the first of the month. Subscribers start on many different dates
- Your cycle runs for 20 weekday service days from your start date, then ends

### The saving on RM18 meals

On the monthly plan, **every meal costs RM15, including the meals priced RM18 on the daily plan**. A daily customer pays RM18 for a Thursday claypot chicken. A monthly subscriber pays RM15 for the same meal. This is the main financial benefit of the monthly plan.

Across a typical 20 meal cycle the rotation contains several RM18 meals, so a monthly subscriber pays RM300 for meals that would cost more than RM300 if bought day by day.

### Weekends and public holidays

Your 20 meals are weekday meals only. Saturday and Sunday are not service days and are never counted against your 20 meals.

**Public holidays are not counted against your 20 meals either.** When a public holiday falls inside your cycle, there is no delivery that day and your cycle is **extended by one service day** so that you still receive all 20 meals you paid for.

Example: a subscriber starts on Monday 24 August 2026 on the lunch plan. Merdeka Day falls on Monday 31 August 2026, a normal service day. There is no delivery on 31 August, no meal is deducted, and the cycle simply runs one weekday longer at the end.

Because start dates differ and holidays fall in different places in each cycle, subscribers finish on many different dates. Two people who started on the same day finish together, but anyone starting a day apart, or crossing a different number of holidays, ends on a different date. If you want to know your own cycle end date or how many meals you have left, ask us and we will check your record.

A subscriber on both lunch and dinner receives 40 meals in a cycle, 20 lunches and 20 dinners across the same 20 service days, for RM600.

## What both plans include

Every meal on both plans includes seasonal vegetables, a side dish and white rice. Add-ons such as brown rice, herbal soup, dessert and tea are charged separately at the prices in the add-on menu, on both plans.

## Changing between plans

You may move from the daily plan to the monthly plan at any time by starting a new monthly cycle. A monthly cycle in progress runs to its end; we do not convert a partly used monthly cycle back into daily credit.
