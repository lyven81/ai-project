# 好吃厨房 Tasty Kitchen, Public Holiday Service Notice

**This notice overrides the standard delivery schedule for the dates listed below.** On these dates the normal Monday to Friday service does not run, and the normal lunch and dinner delivery windows do not apply.

## Closure dates

### Monday 31 August 2026, Merdeka Day (Hari Kebangsaan)
- No lunch delivery
- No dinner delivery
- The kitchen is closed
- Service resumes as normal on Tuesday 1 September 2026

### Wednesday 16 September 2026, Malaysia Day (Hari Malaysia)
- No lunch delivery
- No dinner delivery
- The kitchen is closed
- Service resumes as normal on Thursday 17 September 2026

## Effect on monthly subscribers

Public holidays are **not deducted** from your 20 meal balance. For each closure date that falls inside your cycle, your cycle is **extended by one service day**.

Worked example. A subscriber on the lunch monthly plan starts on Monday 24 August 2026. Without any holiday, 20 weekday meals would end on Friday 18 September 2026. Two public holidays fall inside that span, Merdeka Day on 31 August and Malaysia Day on 16 September, so no meals are delivered on those two days and none are deducted. The cycle extends by two service days and ends on Tuesday 22 September 2026 instead.

Because subscribers start on different dates, the number of holidays inside each cycle differs. Ask us and we will confirm your own adjusted end date.

## Effect on daily customers

There is nothing to adjust. Simply do not place an order for a closure date. Any order mistakenly placed for a closure date is held as credit against your next order.

## Order cut-off around a closure

The 5:00pm cut-off still applies, and it counts service days, not calendar days. To receive lunch on Tuesday 1 September 2026, the day after Merdeka Day, order by 5:00pm on Friday 28 August 2026, because Saturday, Sunday and Merdeka Day are all non-service days.

## Dates still to be confirmed

Maulidur Rasul falls on a lunar date and the 2026 date has not yet been confirmed against the official Selangor gazette. If it lands on a weekday service day, a further notice will be issued and the same extension rule will apply.
