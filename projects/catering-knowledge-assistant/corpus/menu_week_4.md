# 好吃厨房 Tasty Kitchen, Week 4 Menu

Service dates: Monday 24 August 2026 to Friday 28 August 2026.
Rotation position: Week 4 of a repeating 4-week cycle.

Week summary: 2 chicken meals, 7 pork meals, 1 tofu meal. There is no fish dish this week. Daily plan price is RM18 on Wednesday dinner, Thursday lunch and Thursday dinner, and RM15 on every other meal. Monthly plan price is RM15 on every meal.

Every meal includes seasonal vegetables (时蔬), a side dish (配菜) and white rice (白饭) at no extra cost.
