# 好吃厨房 Tasty Kitchen, Business and Service Information

## Who we are

好吃厨房 Tasty Kitchen is a home-style Chinese meal delivery service operating in Shah Alam and Kota Kemuning, Selangor. We cook fresh every weekday morning and deliver lunch and dinner to homes.

Our promise, as printed on every menu:
- 新鲜烹调·每日配送, freshly cooked, delivered daily
- 均衡营养·美味健康, balanced nutrition, tasty and healthy
- 真材实料·吃得安心, real ingredients, eat with peace of mind

## Contact

- **Enquiry hours:** 9:00am to 5:00pm, Monday to Friday
- **Weekend enquiries:** we do not answer messages on Saturday or Sunday. Messages sent over the weekend are answered on the next working day.

Please note that enquiry hours end at 5:00pm, which is the same time as the order cut-off. An order enquiry sent after 5:00pm will be handled the next working day and will apply to the following service day.

## How the service works

1. Check the weekly menu for the day you want
2. Place your order by 5:00pm the day before
3. Pay by online bank transfer and send the receipt
4. Your meal arrives ready to eat inside the delivery window

## The menu rotation

The menu runs on a repeating **4-week rotation**. After Week 4 the cycle returns to Week 1. Each meal within a rotation is different from the others, so a customer eating with us every day does not repeat a dish inside four weeks.

Each week offers ten meals, a lunch and a dinner for each of the five weekdays.

## Dietary scope

Please read this section before ordering if you have dietary requirements.

- **Halal:** we are **not halal certified**. Pork is cooked in the same kitchen and appears on the menu most days.
- **Pork:** pork is the most common protein on our menu. Across the 4-week rotation of 40 meals, 21 are pork dishes.
- **Chicken:** 15 of the 40 meals in the rotation are chicken dishes.
- **Fish:** 3 of the 40 meals are fish dishes. Fish is served on Wednesday dinner in rotation weeks 1, 2 and 3. Week 4 has no fish main dish. The Week 4 Thursday lunch, 咸鱼花腩煲 Claypot Pork Belly with Salted Fish, contains salted fish.
- **Beef:** we do **not** serve beef. There is no beef dish anywhere in the rotation.
- **Vegetarian:** we do **not** offer a vegetarian plan. The only tofu-led main is Stuffed Tofu on Week 4 Wednesday dinner, and it is prepared in a kitchen that handles pork and fish. Mapo Tofu on Week 3 Tuesday lunch contains minced pork despite its name.
- **Soups:** all five herbal soups are pork rib based. There is no chicken or vegetarian soup.
- **Allergens:** we publish the dish name and its main protein on the menu. We do not publish a separate allergen or nutrition breakdown. If you have a specific allergy, contact us before ordering and we will tell you what is in that day's dish.

We cannot substitute a dish, swap a protein or prepare a separate meal to order. The menu is fixed for all customers on a given day.
