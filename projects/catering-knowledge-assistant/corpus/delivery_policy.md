# 好吃厨房 Tasty Kitchen, Delivery Policy

## Delivery areas

Delivery is available in two areas only:

- Shah Alam
- Kota Kemuning

Delivery to both areas is **free of charge**. There is no minimum order value and no delivery fee.

We do not deliver outside Shah Alam and Kota Kemuning. This includes nearby areas such as Klang, Subang Jaya, Puchong, Petaling Jaya, Bukit Jelutong, Cyberjaya and Kuala Lumpur. We do not offer paid delivery, self-collection or third party courier to addresses outside the two service areas. Customers outside the coverage area cannot be served at this time.

## Delivery windows

- **Lunch:** 11:30am to 1:00pm
- **Dinner:** 5:00pm to 6:30pm

Meals are delivered within the stated window. We cannot guarantee an exact arrival time inside the window, as the delivery order depends on the route for that day.

## Service days

Delivery runs Monday to Friday only.

There is no delivery on Saturday or Sunday. There is no delivery on public holidays. See the holiday notice document for the specific dates affected.

## Order cut-off

Orders must be placed **by 5:00pm on the day before delivery**.

Examples:
- To receive lunch on Wednesday, order by 5:00pm on Tuesday.
- To receive dinner on Wednesday, order by 5:00pm on Tuesday.
- To receive a meal on Monday, order by 5:00pm on the previous Friday, since we are closed on the weekend.

Orders placed after the cut-off will be scheduled for the next available service day. We cannot accept same day orders, because meals are cooked fresh to the confirmed order count each morning.

## Changes and cancellations

Any change or cancellation to an order must also reach us before the 5:00pm cut-off on the day before delivery. After the cut-off the meal has been counted into the cooking plan and cannot be changed or cancelled. See the payment and refund policy for what happens to a meal cancelled after cut-off.

## Meal condition on arrival

Meals are delivered ready to eat. No reheating or further preparation is required. We recommend eating the meal shortly after it arrives for the best taste and texture.
