# 好吃厨房 Tasty Kitchen, Frequently Asked Questions

These are the questions customers ask most often on WhatsApp. Where a question involves money, dates or terms, the linked policy document is the authority.

## Ordering

**Q: How do I order?**
Send us a WhatsApp message on 012-345 6789 with the date, the service you want (lunch, dinner or both) and any add-ons. We confirm and send bank details. See business_info.

**Q: What time must I order by?**
By 5:00pm on the day before delivery. See delivery_policy.

**Q: Can I order for today?**
No. We cook to the confirmed order count each morning, so same day orders are not possible. Your order will go to the next service day. See delivery_policy.

**Q: I want a meal on Monday. When do I order?**
By 5:00pm the previous Friday, since we are closed on the weekend. See delivery_policy.

**Q: Can I change or cancel my order?**
Yes, if you tell us before 5:00pm on the day before. After the cut-off the meal is already counted into the cooking plan and cannot be changed. See payment_and_refund_policy.

**Q: Is there a minimum order?**
No. You can order a single meal on the daily plan. See delivery_policy.

## Delivery

**Q: Do you deliver to Shah Alam?**
Yes, and delivery is free. See delivery_policy.

**Q: Do you deliver to Kota Kemuning?**
Yes, and delivery is free. See delivery_policy.

**Q: Do you deliver to Klang, Subang, Puchong, PJ or KL?**
No. We serve Shah Alam and Kota Kemuning only. We do not offer paid delivery or self-collection outside these two areas. See delivery_policy.

**Q: How much is delivery?**
Free within our two service areas. There is no delivery fee and no minimum order.

**Q: What time will my food arrive?**
Lunch between 11:30am and 1:00pm. Dinner between 5:00pm and 6:30pm. We cannot promise an exact time inside the window. See delivery_policy.

**Q: Do you deliver on weekends?**
No. We deliver Monday to Friday only.

**Q: Do you deliver on public holidays?**
No. See holiday_notice_2026 for the specific dates.

## Menu and food

**Q: What is today's lunch?**
Check the menu for the current rotation week. The menu runs on a repeating 4-week cycle, so any date maps to one of the four weekly menus. See menu_week_1 through menu_week_4.

**Q: Can I choose my own dish?**
No. One lunch dish and one dinner dish are served each day, the same for every customer. The subscription is for convenience, not for menu choice. See subscription_guide.

**Q: What comes with the meal?**
Seasonal vegetables, a side dish and white rice, all included in the price. See addons_and_soups.

**Q: Do I need to reheat the food?**
No. Meals arrive ready to eat. We suggest eating soon after delivery for the best taste.

**Q: How much is brown rice?**
An extra RM2 per meal. See addons_and_soups.

**Q: How much is the soup?**
The herbal pork rib soup is an extra RM13 per serving, and you may pick any of the five varieties. See addons_and_soups.

**Q: How much is dessert?**
An extra RM5 per serving. The dessert changes daily and is not published in advance.

**Q: Do you have halal food?**
No. We are not halal certified, and pork is cooked in the same kitchen. See business_info.

**Q: Do you serve beef?**
No. There is no beef dish anywhere in the menu rotation. See business_info.

**Q: Which meals have no pork?**
Chicken, fish and tofu dishes are marked on each weekly menu with a protein icon. Across the 4-week rotation, 15 meals are chicken, 3 are fish and 1 is tofu. Note that Mapo Tofu contains minced pork, and Claypot Pork Belly with Salted Fish contains both pork and fish. See business_info.

**Q: Do you have a vegetarian option?**
No. We do not offer a vegetarian plan, and the one tofu-led main is prepared in a kitchen that handles pork and fish. See business_info.

**Q: Which meals contain chicken?**
Chicken dishes are marked with a chicken icon on each weekly menu. See menu_week_1 through menu_week_4.

**Q: Show me the fish dishes.**
Fish is served on Wednesday dinner in rotation weeks 1, 2 and 3. Week 4 has no fish main dish, though the Thursday lunch claypot contains salted fish. See the individual weekly menus.

**Q: Can you leave out an ingredient or swap the protein?**
No. The menu is fixed for all customers on a given day and we cannot prepare a separate meal to order. See business_info.

**Q: Do you publish calories or allergens?**
No. We publish the dish name and its main protein. If you have a specific allergy, contact us before ordering and we will tell you what is in that day's dish.

## Plans and pricing

**Q: How much is one meal?**
RM15 or RM18 depending on the meal if you order day by day, as printed on the weekly menu. RM15 on every meal if you are on the monthly plan. See subscription_guide.

**Q: Why are some meals RM18?**
The RM18 meals are usually the claypot or slow-braised dishes, most often on Wednesday and Thursday. The weekly menu prints the price of every meal, so check the menu for the day you want. Daily customers pay the printed price. Monthly subscribers pay RM15 for every meal. See subscription_guide.

**Q: Why is my friend paying RM15 for the claypot chicken and I am paying RM18?**
Your friend is on the monthly plan, which is priced at a flat RM15 per meal, including the meals priced RM18 on the daily plan. You are ordering day by day. See subscription_guide.

**Q: How much is the monthly plan?**
RM300 for 20 weekday meals of one service. Lunch only is RM300, dinner only is RM300, both is RM600. See subscription_guide.

**Q: Can I subscribe for lunch only?**
Yes. Lunch only, dinner only, or both. See subscription_guide.

**Q: Is there a weekly plan?**
No. We offer the daily plan and the monthly plan only. See subscription_guide.

**Q: Can I start the monthly plan in the middle of the month?**
Yes. Your cycle starts on whatever date you choose and runs for 20 weekday meals from there. Subscribers start on many different dates. See subscription_guide.

**Q: When does my monthly cycle end?**
It depends on your start date and on how many public holidays and cancelled meals fall inside your cycle, since each one extends the cycle by a service day. Ask us and we will check your record. See subscription_guide.

**Q: How many meals do I have left?**
Ask us and we will check your record.

**Q: Do weekends use up my 20 meals?**
No. Your 20 meals are weekday meals only. Saturday and Sunday are never counted. See subscription_guide.

**Q: What happens to my meals on a public holiday?**
Nothing is deducted. Your cycle extends by one service day for each holiday. See holiday_notice_2026.

## Payment and refunds

**Q: How do I pay?**
Online bank transfer only. We do not take cash, card or e-wallet. See payment_and_refund_policy.

**Q: Can I pay cash on delivery?**
No. Online transfer only.

**Q: When do I pay?**
Daily plan customers pay when the order is confirmed. Monthly subscribers pay the full cycle before it begins. See payment_and_refund_policy.

**Q: Can I get a refund?**
No. We do not offer refunds in any situation. See payment_and_refund_policy.

**Q: Can I pause my subscription?**
There is no formal pause. Cancel the affected meals before 5:00pm the day before, and each one is not deducted and extends your cycle by a service day. That gives the same result as a pause. Meals not cancelled in time are deducted and are not refunded. See payment_and_refund_policy.

**Q: I was away for a week and did not tell you. Can I get those meals back?**
No. Meals not cancelled before the cut-off are counted as delivered. See payment_and_refund_policy.

Phone or WhatsApp on 012-345 6789, or through our Facebook and Instagram pages.
