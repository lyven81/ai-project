# 好吃厨房 Tasty Kitchen, Payment and Refund Policy

## Accepted payment method

**Online bank transfer only.**

We do not accept cash on delivery, credit card, debit card, e-wallet, cheque or any payment app. Please transfer to the account details we provide when your order is confirmed, and send us the transfer receipt.

## When payment is due

- **Daily plan:** pay per use. Payment is due when the order is confirmed, before the 5:00pm cut-off on the day before delivery.
- **Monthly plan:** the full RM300 for the cycle, or RM600 if you take both lunch and dinner, is paid before the cycle begins.

Add-ons are charged on top and are paid with the order they belong to.

An order is only confirmed once payment is received. An unpaid order is not counted into the cooking plan and will not be delivered.

## Refund policy

**We do not offer refunds.**

This applies to all situations, including:

- A meal you no longer want after the 5:00pm cut-off
- Days you were away, unwell or travelling during a monthly cycle
- Meals left unused at the end of a monthly cycle
- A monthly cycle you wish to stop partway through

Once the cut-off has passed, ingredients for your meal have been bought and the meal has been counted into the cooking plan, so the cost cannot be recovered.

## Cancellation before cut-off

A cancellation received **before 5:00pm on the day before delivery** is free of charge. For daily customers, the payment is held as credit against your next order. For monthly subscribers, the meal is not deducted from your 20 meals and your cycle extends by one service day, exactly as it does for a public holiday.

A cancellation received **after the cut-off** is not possible. The meal is counted as delivered and is deducted from your monthly meal balance.

## Pausing a monthly cycle

There is no formal pause facility. If you know in advance that you will be away, cancel the affected meals before the 5:00pm cut-off for each day. Each meal cancelled in time is not deducted, and your cycle extends by one service day for each cancelled meal. This achieves the same result as a pause.

Cancellations that miss the cut-off cannot be recovered and are not refunded.

## Unused meals at the end of a cycle

A monthly cycle is 20 service days of meals. Because cancelled and holiday days extend the cycle rather than expiring, meals are not normally lost. Any balance remaining when you choose to stop is not refunded and does not carry into a new cycle.
