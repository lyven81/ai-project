# 好吃厨房 Tasty Kitchen, Week 1 Menu

Service dates: Monday 3 August 2026 to Friday 7 August 2026.
Rotation position: Week 1 of a repeating 4-week cycle.

Week summary: 4 chicken meals, 5 pork meals, 1 fish meal. Fish is served on Wednesday dinner. Daily plan price is RM18 on Wednesday lunch, Thursday lunch and Thursday dinner, and RM15 on every other meal. Monthly plan price is RM15 on every meal.

Every meal includes seasonal vegetables (时蔬), a side dish (配菜) and white rice (白饭) at no extra cost.
