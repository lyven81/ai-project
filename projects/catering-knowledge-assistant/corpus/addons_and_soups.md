# 好吃厨房 Tasty Kitchen, Add-on Menu and Soup Selection

Add-ons are optional and apply to every meal in every week of the rotation. Add-on prices are the same for daily customers and monthly subscribers. Add-ons are charged separately and are not included in the RM300 monthly subscription price.

## Add-on prices

### 升级糙米饭 Brown Rice Upgrade
- Price: additional RM2 per meal
- Description: replaces the standard white rice with brown rice
- Availability: every meal, every day

### 滋洞养身排骨汤 Herbal Pork Rib Soup
- Price: additional RM13 per serving
- Description: a nourishing herbal soup made with pork ribs, chosen from the weekly soup selection below
- Availability: every meal, every day
- Note: all soup varieties are pork rib based. There is no chicken or vegetarian soup option.

### 今日甜品 Today's Dessert
- Price: additional RM5 per serving
- Description: the dessert of the day, which changes daily and is not published in advance
- Availability: every meal, every day

### 养身茶 Nourishing Tea
- Price: RM13 per serving
- Description: a herbal wellness tea
- Availability: every meal, every day

## Weekly soup selection

Five herbal pork rib soups are offered on rotation. Customers who order the herbal soup add-on may choose any one of the five.

1. 淮山玉竹排骨汤, Chinese Yam and Solomon's Seal Pork Rib Soup
2. 虫草花四神排骨汤, Cordyceps Flower and Four God Herbs Pork Rib Soup
3. 莲藕花生排骨汤, Lotus Root and Peanut Pork Rib Soup
4. 党参黄芪排骨汤, Dang Shen and Astragalus Pork Rib Soup
5. 罗汉果雪梨排骨汤, Luo Han Guo and Snow Pear Pork Rib Soup

In Week 1 of the rotation, option 4 is served as 党参芡实排骨汤, Dang Shen and Gorgon Fruit Pork Rib Soup, instead of the Astragalus version.

## What is already included at no extra cost

Every main meal already includes seasonal vegetables (时蔬), a side dish (配菜) and white rice (白饭). Customers do not need to pay extra for these. The add-ons above are upgrades on top of the standard meal.
